Linear Algebra: Four Fundamental Subspaces & Dimension Theorem

1. Reduced Row Echelon Form (RREF)

A matrix is in RREF if: (a) all non-zero rows precede all-zero rows; (b) each leading entry of a row is 1; (c) each leading 1 is strictly to the right of the leading 1 in the row above; (d) each leading 1 is the sole non-zero entry in its entire column.

2. Pivot and Free Variables

Columns possessing leading 1s in $\text{RREF}(A)$ are pivot columns, corresponding to basic (bound) variables. Columns devoid of leading 1s are non-pivot columns, corresponding to free variables (parameters).

3. Rank of a Matrix

$\text{Rank}(A)$ is strictly defined as the number of pivot columns in $\text{RREF}(A)$, which identically equals the dimension of the column space (range) of $A$.

4. Null Space (Kernel) & Nullity

The null space is $\text{Null}(A) = \{\mathbf{x} \in \mathbb{R}^n \mid A\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{0}\}$. $\text{Nullity}(A) = \dim(\text{Null}(A)) = \text{number of free variables}$. Each basis vector satisfies $A\mathbf{v} = \mathbf{0}$. If there are no free variables, $\text{Null}(A) = \{\mathbf{0}\}$ and $\text{Nullity}(A) = 0$.

5. Range (Column Space) Basis

The range $\text{Range}(A) = \text{Col}(A)$ is the span of the columns of $A$. Crucial Theorem: A basis for $\text{Range}(A)$ is formed by the pivot columns of the original matrix $A$, never by the columns of $\text{RREF}(A)$, because elementary row operations alter column spaces.

6. Rank-Nullity Theorem (The Dimension Theorem)

For any linear map $T: \mathbb{R}^n \to \mathbb{R}^m$ represented by an $m \times n$ matrix $A$: $$\text{Rank}(A) + \text{Nullity}(A) = n$$ The total dimension of domain $\mathbb{R}^n$ is conserved: exactly $\text{Rank}(A)$ dimensions map into the image in $\mathbb{R}^m$, while $\text{Nullity}(A)$ dimensions are collapsed to the zero vector.
